### Illustrative sensitivities

The inputs are analyst assumptions. All other revenues, fixed costs and depreciation are held constant.

- **Sugar**. Starting volume 100,000 t, selling price EUR 500/t and variable cost EUR 420/t. A -10% volume change and EUR +0/t price change give EUR -5,000,000 revenue and EUR -800,000 operating result, all else fixed.

- **Starch**. Starting volume 10,000 t, selling price EUR 500/t and variable cost EUR 420/t. A +5% volume change and EUR -20/t price change give EUR +40,000 revenue and EUR -170,000 operating result, all else fixed.
