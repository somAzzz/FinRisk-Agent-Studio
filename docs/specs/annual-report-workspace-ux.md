# Annual report workspace: research, exposures and evidence

Design proposal · English product copy · No production UI changes

## The workflow to design for

An analyst opens a saved company report, finds the segment that explains the group movement, follows a related alert, checks the original and returns to the same row. A reviewer can reproduce what was knowable on the selected date and explain an abstention. The report should remain useful even when there are no confirmed product relationships.

Keep Today, Companies, Research Runs and Journal. Add **Annual reports** to Companies; allow a direct link even without a standard FinRisk run. A report index lists fiscal year, issuer, publication date, available study runs and latest evidence cutoff. The existing launcher still accepts a URL or upload. It should create a saved run with progress and a link to the report; opening existing results must not trigger inference. Reuse the existing navigation, restrained teal accents, typography, table styles and evidence-drawer interaction.

## Screen and navigation contract

| View | First impression | Primary action and destination |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Company, fiscal year, publication date and active evidence cutoff; one sourced conclusion; a segment comparison table; what remains unresolved. A separately labelled later-evidence section follows the annual baseline. | Select Sugar to inspect its segment numbers and sources; choose “View related alerts” to open Tracking at the relevant date and run. |
| Financials & operations | Five segments plus the group; revenue / operating EBITDA / operating result switcher; prior, current, delta, units. On this report Sugar's −164 of the group's −187 million change explains 87.7% of the net decline. Show calculation and scope, not an event attribution. | Select a value to open the underlying row. Present the profit bridge and cash/working-capital disclosures separately. A searchable facts table exposes all 128 extracted rows with section, periods and source; duplicate disclosures on different pages are not summed. |
| Relationships | A labelled path list and an optional graph of the same scoped dataset. Start with a selected segment or alert, not the entire network. Offer “Business relationships” and “Unconfirmed candidates” as distinct views. | Select a path to focus it; select an edge to inspect its exact predicate, origin, supporting passages and limitations. |
| Tracking | Two distinct run lanes: **Fresh automated tracking** and **Reviewed case replay**. Choose a dated snapshot. Show new alerts, previously known alerts and still-open questions separately. | Open an alert for its product, segment, H1 reported changes, attribution status and evidence. Open a task to inspect the search, accepted originals, evidence gaps and audit chain. |
| Evidence & review | The 8 supported findings lead; review queue shows 4 insufficient, 1 wrong citation and 1 partial. Show open follow-ups and uncovered topics separately. | Expand a claim to see original proposal, revisions, decisive span, reason, and outstanding evidence. Viewing a rejected claim never promotes it. |

Use a persistent **English report reader** for the reviewed narrative. Label analyst synthesis and any separately checked policy sources explicitly; do not present prose as output of the automated auditor. Link narrative references into the appropriate view. Keep raw JSON/downloads as an optional inspection tool.

The **page/date context** is a report identity plus active source run, cutoff, period and selected item. Preserve these in the URL/back stack. Switching cutoffs retains the selection only if eligible; otherwise explain the absence and offer the prior selection on return. May has a fresh snapshot but no reviewed case snapshot here: show “No reviewed snapshot for this date”, never backfill from October. Separate issuer publication date, financial period, event date (if known), and alert detection date. Example: July 9 forecast, October 8 alert, March–August H1 figures.

## How the relationship views work

| Source in this case | Representation | Restrictions |
|---|---|---|
| Reviewed November exposure snapshot: 34 nodes / 38 edges, 17 source records | Company/segment/product/site/region/customer class in the business view. Metrics, alerts, policy observations and scenarios appear when following a specific path. Existing edge origins: 16 annual disclosures, 8 interim disclosures, 7 external confirmations, 7 model inferences. | “Reviewed case” is the origin of the dataset, not the proof type of every edge. The 38 edges include tracking, comparison and scenario links; do not label all 38 supply-chain facts. Customer classes are markets, not named buyers. |
| Annual-only findings: 2 supply-chain links | Show the reported physical or commercial flows with the supporting finding. Example: the fruit businesses sell to dairy, ice-cream and beverage customer classes. | The source establishes a relationship at that scope; fulfilment is unknown. Do not merge these labels into canonical replay nodes without verified entity alignment. |
| Unmapped product audit: 28 distinct unknown candidates, 0 supported | Separate list searchable by product or proposed segment. Each row shows source page, extracted wording, reason and missing evidence. Explain the formal-segment-note matching failure once. | No factual traversal from a missing segment. No shared “Unknown segment” hub. No invented segment, supplier or confidence score. |
| Fresh tracking audit graph | In the audit drawer, show a short ordered trail: task, proposal, attempted revision, verdict, decisive passage, dated document or follow-up. | A task citing a report is evidence lineage, not a business relationship. Bind to the fresh run only. |

**Path example.** Select the October beet alert in the reviewed run. The alert concerns sugar beet; the Sugar segment uses sugar beet; that segment reported an H1 operating-result change of **+EUR 26m** (−89 → −63). Display the exact edge verbs, including the direction of USES even when reading the path backwards. Label the last node “Reported segment change”. Beside it show “Impact attributable to this alert: Unknown” and “Contract fulfilment: Unknown”. Do not draw a causal arrow to the metric. A Wabern permit can establish the operator and beet-processing activity, but not a regional delivery route or current utilisation.

**Graph behaviour.** Default to evidenced links for one selected entity. Expand one hop at a time, reveal a count before expanding busy nodes, and retain the focus/zoom when the source drawer closes. Filter by entity type, relationship, provenance and date. Use solid labelled links for disclosed or independently evidenced relationships, distinct text labels/icons for the source, and dashed links for optional **Model inference**. Inferred edges and scenario paths are off by default and never count as verified paths. Edge width is constant: there are no reliable flow volumes for a Sankey. Show disconnected candidates and “No supported paths at this date” without implying no economic exposure.

**Evidence drawer.** Show the verbatim source passage or supplied anchors, document, source category, publication date, physical PDF page and printed page when available. Show a calculation with input rows for derived facts. Offer “Open original at page”; if a local PDF has no accessible URL, use an authorised document endpoint or show the citation and explain that the file is unavailable. HTML has no PDF page. Only highlight exact coordinates when provided. For compound chains list the evidence for each step, not one citation for the entire path. Modal focus returns to the opening edge/cell; on narrow screens use a full-screen sheet and a path table instead of requiring pan and zoom. All filters and rows must be keyboard accessible; status must not rely on colour.

## Other extracted results

- **Chart extraction:** a small paired-year bar or dot plot per measure and an accessible table. Keep hectares, million tonnes and tonnes/ha on separate scales; preserve published percentage changes rather than recomputing from rounded values. Example: total sugar output 3.9 → 3.8 million tonnes, published −2.8%. Show PDF p. 45 / printed p. 43 and the extraction method on demand.
- **Policy:** a DE / FR / EU comparison by cutoff, with stage, evidence date, effective date if established, source and product exposure. A proposal is not in force; an EU coordination measure is not a common tax. The German policy task was assigned. Distinguish it from machine-selected tasks and from the analyst's separate Mercosur source check.
- **Actuals, attribution, scenario:** use three distinct sections sharing a period/unit header only when appropriate. Provide scenario assumptions with the result; if interactive edits are added later, mark the result a local simulation and supply a reset. Never fill missing attribution with the scenario result.
- **Data quality:** expose four reconciliation checks, extraction coverage, market-source eligibility, and review history on demand. Label OCR only on the pages that actually used it. A passed reconciliation validates arithmetic, not every claim. No sources, missing module, zero alerts, zero monetary value, and unknown impact require different wording. Do not show empty XBRL, plant-NPV or market-series charts as zero-valued results.
- **Static demo:** if a sample is provided, label it “Archived sample”, with working read-only links within that sample. Keep demo data separate from real results. Do not substitute another company's financials or graph.

## Delivery plan and acceptance

| Increment | Data and component work | Acceptance using Südzucker |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Reopen and read | Add a persisted registry and read API for a scoped report bundle, including source document access. Ingest the existing archived results without rerunning the LLM. Use a company report route, persistent overview, segment table and reusable evidence drawer. | Refresh or reopen the same run and row; show 5 segments and the group; reconcile group operating result to 163 and 350; reach the source of −164 from the summary in two actions. |
| 2. Inspect a relationship | Add scoped graph adapters and reuse React Flow rendering from the existing evidence experience. Keep business links, audit lineage and unresolved candidates separate. Add keyboard-equivalent path table. No Neo4j dependency for reading a sealed snapshot. | Trace the beet alert to Sugar and the reported +26; no attributed amount; toggle seven inference edges in the November snapshot without changing the factual paths; the 28 unknown candidates have no verified segment path. |
| 3. Compare what was known | Add cutoff and run-lane comparison, alert identity, policy states, claim revision histories and optional scenario detail. Link legacy Evidence and Research Runs back to the saved report. | Fresh tracking shows 0 new alerts for May, October and November. Reviewed October shows 2; November retains those 2, with no duplicate new alert. May cannot show the October H1 report. A rejected claim remains inspectable. Direct links and browser Back restore the selected snapshot and originating row. |

Suggested read model keys: report ID, company ID, fiscal period, seed fingerprint, run ID, run type and cutoff. Store lineage per item: evidence ID(s), date, page/anchor, dataset and source role. Existing annual-report POST endpoints run analysis; they are not a report archive. Import a manifest of authorised artifact IDs server-side rather than exposing arbitrary filesystem paths. Derive group comparison numbers and status counts from the selected payload. Do not join by display name alone. Indicate per module whether it is available, unbuilt, failed, or produced no results.
